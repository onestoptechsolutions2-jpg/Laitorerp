index.global.min.js as the "global" build of the `fullcalendar` npm package, v6.1.15) are
distributed under the MIT license. Confirmed via the published package metadata

This vendored copy deliberately excludes FullCalendar's separately-licensed Premium/Scheduler
plugins (resource views, timeline views) - those require a separate commercial license and are
not needed for this app's calendar (view + drag/reassign of standalone events, plus a read-only
